# Local Studio

![](media:local-studio-tour)

## The brief

Brand, website and app for Local Studio, private AI that runs on your Mac.

Local Studio runs AI models on your Mac. It needed a brand and a website.

## The website

I designed the brand and built the website. The site shows the app, the setup of coding models and the phone screens.

## The app

Since August 2026 I work on Local Studio with its founder, Sherif Cherfa (0xSero), at [Sybil Solutions](https://www.sybilsolutions.ai/). I rebuilt the app as native Mac and Linux software: one shared core, and a thin native interface on each system in place of a web page in a desktop shell.

## The sponsorship

I also designed the local AI dashboards for Omarchy, the Linux distribution by DHH. This work helped win Sybil Solutions a [three-year premier sponsorship](https://omarchy.org/news/2026/09/omacom-foundation-to-be-premier-sponsor-of-0xsero/) from the Omacom Foundation.
