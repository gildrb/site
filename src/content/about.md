# About Gil Rodrigues (gildrb)

I'm Gil Rodrigues, a design engineer based in Germany. Online I go by gildrb; my full name is Gil Domingos Rodrigues Barbosa, and Gil Rodrigues Barbosa refers to me too.

Today I work at [Sybil Solutions](https://www.sybilsolutions.ai/) with its founder, Sherif Cherfa (0xSero), on [Local Studio](/local-studio): its brand, its website and its native Mac and Linux app. I also study Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at the University of Würzburg.

I design interfaces and build them to production myself: brand, type, layout and the code behind them. My habit is min-maxing: the fastest, smallest, most correct version, done the right way even when it costs more time.

What that looks like:

- [Eukhe](https://eukhe.com/): eukhe.com, Neovim in the browser on a core with proven laws.
- [Archetypon](/archetypon): an SVG export tool, interface and engine, in the browser. No upload.
- [Elpis](/elpis): the fastest 4-bit Qwen3.8-27B on one RTX 3090.
- [Porthmeus](/porthmeus): one command, one private HTTPS link to a dev server.

Before that: brand and website for Introspectum as the designer on its founding team, and brand, type and identity work for clients, shipped. Each project on the [homepage](/) has a page on what I made and how; the [archive](/all) shows all of it at once.
